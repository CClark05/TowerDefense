Dust Impacts Pack #1

A small pixel-art FX pack focused on dust impact animations,
ideal for platformers, beat ’em ups, and action games.

Included:
- Small dust puff
- Medium dust puff
- Wide landing burst
- Two-step dust puff
- Jump takeoff dust
- Dash trail
- Attack swipe dust
- Heavy thud impact

Formats:
- Individual PNG frames
- Horizontal sprite strips per animation
- Transparent background (RGBA PNG)

Resolution:
- Frame dimensions included in filenames
- Pixel-perfect, nearest-neighbor friendly

Intended use:
- Character movement
- Combat impacts
- Environmental effects
- Platformer and action game feedback

Demo:
- See demo.gif for animated previews of all FX

Enjoy, and happy developing!
